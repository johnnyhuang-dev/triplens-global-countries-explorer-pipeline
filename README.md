## Triplens Global Countries Explorer Pipeline

Ever wondered which country to travel to for your next vacation plans? Perhaps a hidden gem on the other side of the planet? This repository contains my work on Triplens, a global countries explorer for tourists looking for their next destination. 

It's and end-to-end data pipeline that gathers essential information on your favorite countries, as well as all other not-so-well-known places that might pique your interest. It makes use of Python, DBT, Apache Airflow, and Snowflake to collect and transform country data from multiple sources.
